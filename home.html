About the program

This internal training accelerates our delivery by aligning teams on DevOps fundamentals and advanced practices. Expect short lectures, live demos, and guided labs mapped to our internal tooling and environments.

  • Outcomes: shipping faster, safer, and with confidence.
  • Audience: engineers, SREs, QA, infra, security, PMs.
  • Format: hybrid (live + self-paced), labs, capstone.
  • Prereqs: basic Git, scripting, cloud fundamentals.

Curriculum

Week 1 — DevOps Culture & Git

  • Flow, feedback, continuous learning
  • Git branching, PRs, code reviews
  • Hands-on: repos, hooks, protected branches

Week 2 — CI/CD Essentials

  • Pipelines, runners, artifacts
  • Hands-on: build & test matrix
  • Deploy strategies: blue/green, canary

Week 3 — Containers & K8s

  • Dockerfiles, image scanning
  • Kubernetes basics, Helm
  • Hands-on: app -> cluster

Week 4 — IaC & Environments

  • Terraform modules, state, drift
  • Secrets & config management
  • Hands-on: staging env pipeline

Week 5 — Observability

  • Metrics, logs, traces (OTel)
  • SLOs, error budgets
  • Hands-on: dashboards & alerts

Week 6 — DevSecOps & Capstone

  • Threat modeling, supply chain
  • SBOMs, policy-as-code
  • Capstone: CI/CD to prod-like env

FAQ

Who can enroll?

All full-time employees and long-term contractors. Prior experience with Git and a programming language is recommended.

What’s the time commitment?

Expect ~4–6 hours/week across lectures, labs, and the capstone project.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes—on completing all labs and the capstone, you’ll receive a company-recognized certificate and an internal badge.
